Version 1.6: formatting at a tap, Quick Look previews of TextBundles, and EPUBs that carry their author, language, series and cover.

FORMATTING
• On iPhone, a bar above the keyboard: Bold, Italic, Code, Link, a Heading button that steps from heading 1 to 2 to 3 and back to plain text, Bullet List, Task List, Quote, Outdent and Indent.
• On iPad, a Format menu in the toolbar holds all of them, plus Strikethrough and every heading level from 1 to 6.
• With a hardware keyboard: Command-B, Command-I and Command-K for bold, italic and a link, Shift-Command-C for code, Shift-Command-X for strikethrough, Option-Command-1 to 6 for headings, Shift-Command-L and Shift-Command-T for the lists, Command-' for a quote, and Command-] and Command-[ to indent and outdent.
• Bold, Italic, Code, Strikethrough, the headings, the lists and Quote toggle: use Bold on bold text and it comes off again. Link inserts link syntax, and Indent and Outdent add and remove indentation. Every command is a single undo step.
• A selection is rewritten with care: around the spaces at its edges, inside text that is already bold and italic, and across several lines at once.
• Typing *, _, a backtick or $ with text selected now wraps the selection instead of replacing it. Wrap Selection When Typing in the toolbar's Typing menu turns that off.

QUICK LOOK
• A TextBundle tapped in Files, or opened from a Mail attachment or by any app that uses Quick Look, now appears as md draws it, on md's page with its math and Graphviz diagrams, instead of as a generic file icon.
• Mermaid and PlantUML are too heavy for a quick look, so their diagrams show as source, with a caption saying md draws them when the file is opened.
• A preview never touches the network. A remote image is left out, not fetched.
• Plain Markdown, PlantUML and Graphviz files and TextPack archives keep the preview iOS gives them itself, because the system does not pass those types to an app.

EPUB DETAILS AND COVER
• Export as EPUB now reads the document's front matter: author, language, date, description, series and series_index join the title in the book's details, where e-book readers show and sort by them. Until now every md EPUB carried its title and nothing else, and always said English.
• A cover line puts an image on the book: a file next to the document, an https image, or an image written inline as data, in PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP. A cover md cannot read is skipped, and the book goes out without one.
• An https cover is the one new thing md fetches. The export downloads that single image, follows at most five redirects and only to https, and gives up after 15 seconds or 20 MB.

FIXES
• Very large PlantUML diagrams, wider or taller than 4096 pixels such as a sequence diagram with fifty participants, now render instead of stopping at "Diagram too large for browser rendering".
• A quote, ampersand or angle bracket followed by a combining accent is now always escaped, so such text can no longer break the page's markup in the preview, the printout, the exports or Quick Look.
